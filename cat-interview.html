The Cat in the Chat: An Interview

By Griff Harpoon · September 14, 2026

The transfer window has been open for twenty-five hours. Recruitment posts scroll past at a rate that suggests several states have confused this channel with a personal ads section and at least one has confused it with a crime. Amid the NAP agreements and bear schedules, one figure has appeared more often than any recruiter, any moderator, and any reasonable explanation for what is happening in this channel: a small cat holding signs.

The cat has no verified state affiliation, no known power level, and no apparent interest in Whiteout Survival as a game. It holds signs. The signs are unprintable. It does not stop. This correspondent requested a formal interview. The cat agreed, or at least it appeared again, which in transfer chat passes for consent.

Griff: Let's start at the beginning. Every career has an origin story, even the ones that probably shouldn't exist. Why did you start doing this?

Griff: You've been out here for twenty-five hours straight holding signs that would get a person removed from most public spaces. Do your arms get tired?

Griff: This correspondent has watched you cycle through approximately forty signs in the last day, and not one of them has contained a bear schedule, a NAP level, or anything resembling useful information. Have you ever considered holding a different sign?

Griff: Everybody's got to make a living, even in transfer chat. You show up, you hold the signs, you leave. Or you don't leave. It's unclear. How do you get paid?

Griff: When the transfer window closes and the last recruitment post sinks to the bottom of the channel, where do you go? Is there a life outside of this?

Griff: Do the moderators know you're here? Has anyone in any official capacity ever acknowledged what is happening in this channel?

Griff: This correspondent has received complaints. Multiple states have flagged your signs as inappropriate, offensive, and in one case "an act of war." Have you ever been reported?

Griff: Let me rephrase. Do you have any kind of permit, license, or authorization to be doing what you are doing in this channel?

Griff: Sources indicate there are multiple cats operating in transfer chat using the same sign format, the same face, and the same complete disregard for community standards. Are you the same cat every time, or are there several of you?

A different cat appeared. This one was wearing a bow.

Griff: In your entire career — and this correspondent is using that word as loosely as it has ever been used — what is the worst sign you've ever held?

Griff: Do you have anything to say for yourself?

Griff: Any final words for the people of transfer chat?

This correspondent has requested reassignment. The request is pending.

—Griff Harpoon, actually reporting.
